# CALIBRATE OS — Vision

---

## Mission

To give every person the clarity, tools, and intelligence to perform at the highest level their biology allows.

---

## Vision

CALIBRATE OS is the operating system for the human body.

Just as a computer operating system manages hardware resources, schedules processes, and runs applications — CALIBRATE OS manages your biological inputs (sleep, nutrition, training, stress, recovery), synthesizes them into a unified performance picture, and runs the right interventions at the right time.

The goal is not to add another health app to someone's phone. The goal is to replace the chaos of fragmented trackers, spreadsheets, and guesswork with a single intelligent system that knows you, learns from you, and continuously improves your performance over time.

---

## Problem Statement

Human performance is a systems problem. Sleep affects recovery. Recovery affects training output. Training output affects nutrition demand. Nutrition demand affects sleep quality. Every variable is connected — yet today's tools treat each one in isolation.

The current landscape fails users in three specific ways:

**1. Fragmentation.** People track sleep in one app, workouts in another, food in a third, and HRV on a wearable. There is no single source of truth. Insights that require connecting these data points never surface.

**2. Passive tracking without action.** Most health apps show you data but do not tell you what to do with it. A resting heart rate chart does not tell you whether to train hard or rest tomorrow. Users are left to interpret their own data without the expertise to do so.

**3. Generic recommendations.** The fitness industry runs on one-size-fits-all programs. But a 28-year-old athlete in a training block has completely different needs than a 45-year-old executive managing chronic stress. Generic advice produces generic results.

## Target Users

**Primary — The Optimizing Athlete**
- Age 22–40, trains 4–6x per week
- Already uses at least one tracker (Garmin, WHOOP, Apple Watch, Oura)
- Frustrated that their tools don't talk to each other
- Willing to pay for real performance gains
- Measures success by PRs, race times, and how they feel

**Secondary — The Performance Professional**
- Age 30–50, high-output knowledge worker or executive
- Views physical health as a competitive advantage at work
- Tracks energy, focus, and stress as much as fitness metrics
- Values time efficiency — needs the "what should I do today" answer fast

**Tertiary — The Biohacker / Health Optimizer**
- Deep interest in longevity, metabolic health, hormones
- Already reads research, self-experiments
- Wants a system that can handle complexity and nuance
- Will be an early adopter and vocal advocate if the product delivers

---

## North Star Metric

**Weekly Performance Loop Completion Rate**

The percentage of active users who complete their full daily performance loop — check-in, training/nutrition log, and review of their AI daily brief — at least 5 out of 7 days in a given week.

This metric captures whether CALIBRATE OS has become the user's operating system for their day, not just an app they occasionally open. High loop completion correlates with data quality, which feeds better AI recommendations, which increases perceived value, which drives retention.

Secondary metrics tracked alongside the North Star:
- 30-day retention rate
- Average daily active sessions per user
- AI recommendation acceptance rate (user acts on what CALIBRATE suggests)
- Net Promoter Score (NPS)
